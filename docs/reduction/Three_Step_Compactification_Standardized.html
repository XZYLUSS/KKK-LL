The Logical Hierarchy

1

Prove existence of a stabilized breathing mode $b_\star$ using only Lovelock gravity. No fluxes. This establishes that the geometric compactification is self-consistent.

Add quantized 6-form flux to tune $\Lambda_{\text{tot}}(b_\star)$ to the desired value. $G_6$ is "clean" because $\Lambda_{\text{flux}}$ is $b$-independent — it shifts vacuum energy without destabilizing.

3

Only after Steps 1+2, add YM flux and/or $G_4$ for gauge symmetry breaking, chiral fermions, Standard Model physics. These create anisotropic pressures — handle with care.

STEP 1: Pure Lovelock Vacuum Stabilization

§1.1 Geometric Setup and Conventions

Definition 1.1 (Manifold Structure)
$$M_{16} = X_4 \times Y_{12}, \quad Y_{12} = \CP^2_1 \times \CP^2_2 \times \CP^2_3$$

Dimensions: $4 + 4 + 4 + 4 = 16$ ✓

Definition 1.2 (Single Breathing Mode Ansatz)
$$ds^2_{16} = g^{(4)}_{\mu\nu}(x)\,dx^\mu dx^\nu + b^2 \sum_{i=1}^{3} \hat{g}^{(i)}_{a_i b_i}(y_i)\,dy^{a_i} dy^{b_i}$$

where $\hat{g}^{(i)}$ is the unit-radius Fubini-Study metric on $\CP^2_i$.

Convention 1.3 (Module 03A Baseline)

We fix the Fubini-Study normalization:

Einstein condition (unit radius):

$$\boxed{\Ric(\hat{g}) = 6\hat{g}, \quad \hat{R} = 24}$$

Volume (unit radius):

$$\boxed{\Vol(\CP^2, \hat{g}) = \frac{\pi^2}{2}}$$

Kähler form period:

$$\boxed{\int_{\CP^1}\omega = \pi}$$

Cohomology relation:

$$\frac{\omega}{2\pi} = \frac{1}{2}H \quad \text{where } H \in H^2(\CP^2, \mathbb{Z})$$
Warning: Literature Discrepancy

Some references use $\Vol(\CP^2) = 2\pi^2$ with $\int_{\CP^1}\omega = 2\pi$. This differs from our baseline by a factor of 4 in volume. All formulas here use $\Vol = \pi^2/2$.

§1.5 Existence and Stability

Theorem 1.7 (Einstein Frame Potential)
$$V_E(b) = \frac{\Lambda_{\text{grav}}(b)}{M_4^4(b)}$$

Extremum condition: $V_E'(b_\star) = 0$

Stability condition: $V_E''(b_\star) > 0$

Key Result: Pure Lovelock Stabilization

For generic Lovelock couplings with appropriate sign choices, $V_E(b)$ has a minimum at some $b_\star > 0$, giving a stabilized breathing mode without requiring any flux.

STEP 2: Quantized Internal $G_6$ — Vacuum Energy Tuning

§2.1 The 6-Form Field Strength

Definition 2.1 (6-Form Action)
$$S_{G_6} = -\frac{1}{2 \cdot 6!}\int_{M_{16}}d^{16}x\sqrt{-g}\,G_{M_1\cdots M_6}G^{M_1\cdots M_6}$$
Theorem 2.2 (Flux Quantization)
$$\frac{1}{(2\pi)^5}\int_{\Sigma_6}G_6 = N \in \mathbb{Z}$$
Result: Flux Contribution to Vacuum Energy
$$\boxed{\Lambda_{\text{flux}} = \frac{\pi^2 N^2}{360}}$$

This is independent of $b$ — crucial for clean stabilization.

§2.2 Why $G_6$ is "Clean"

Key Property: $b$-Independence
$$\boxed{\begin{aligned} \Lambda_{\text{tot}}(b) &= \Lambda_{\text{grav}}(b) + \Lambda_{\text{flux}}\\[8pt] \Lambda_{\text{tot}}'(b) &= \Lambda_{\text{grav}}'(b)\\[8pt] \Lambda_{\text{tot}}''(b) &= \Lambda_{\text{grav}}''(b) \end{aligned}}$$

The $G_6$ flux does not affect the location of $b_\star$!

It only shifts the vacuum energy value at that extremum.

§2.3 Vacuum Energy Tuning

Theorem 2.3 (Vacuum Tuning)

Given $b_\star$ from Step 1:

  • Minkowski: Choose $N$ such that $\Lambda_{\text{grav}}(b_\star) + \frac{\pi^2 N^2}{360} = 0$
  • dS: Choose $N$ such that $\Lambda_{\text{grav}}(b_\star) + \frac{\pi^2 N^2}{360} > 0$
  • AdS: Choose $N$ such that $\Lambda_{\text{grav}}(b_\star) + \frac{\pi^2 N^2}{360} < 0$

§3.2 Yang-Mills Flux $F^{(i)} = f_i\omega_i$

Definition 3.1 (Kähler Flux Ansatz)
$$F^{(i)} = f_i\,\omega_i$$
Theorem 3.2 (Kähler Contractions)
$$\omega_{ac}\omega_b{}^c = +g_{ab}, \quad \omega_{ab}\omega^{ab} = 4$$ $$|F^{(i)}|^2 = 4f_i^2$$
Theorem 3.3 (Flux Quantization — Corrected)

With baseline normalization $\int_{\CP^1}\omega = \pi$:

$$\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{\CP^1}F^{(i)} = \frac{f_i}{2\pi} \cdot \pi = \frac{f_i}{2} \in \mathbb{Z}$$ $$\boxed{f_i = 2n_i, \quad n_i \in \mathbb{Z}}$$
YM Stress-Energy Summary
$$\boxed{\begin{aligned} T^{(i)}_{ab}\big|_{\CP^2_i} &= 0 \quad \text{(self block)}\\[6pt] T^{(i)}_{\mu\nu} &= -\frac{f_i^2}{g_{16}^2}g_{\mu\nu} \quad \text{(external)}\\[6pt] T^{(i)}_{ab}\big|_{\CP^2_{j\neq i}} &= -\frac{f_i^2}{g_{16}^2}g_{ab} \quad \text{(other internal)} \end{aligned}}$$
$b$-Dependence of YM
$$|F^{(i)}|^2 = \frac{4f_i^2}{b^4}, \quad \Lambda_{\text{YM}}(b) \sim \sum_{i=1}^{3}\frac{f_i^2}{g_{16}^2 b^4}$$

This is $b$-dependent and affects $b_\star$!
