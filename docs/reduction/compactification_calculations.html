4. Fubini-Study Geometry on $\mathbb{CP}^2$

4.1 Complex Coordinates on $\mathbb{CP}^2$

$\mathbb{CP}^2$ is the space of complex lines in $\mathbb{C}^3$:

$$\mathbb{CP}^2 = \frac{\mathbb{C}^3 \setminus \{0\}}{\mathbb{C}^*}$$

Homogeneous coordinates: $[Z^0 : Z^1 : Z^2]$ with equivalence $(Z^0, Z^1, Z^2) \sim \lambda(Z^0, Z^1, Z^2)$ for $\lambda \in \mathbb{C}^*$.

Local coordinates on patch $U_0 = \{Z^0 \neq 0\}$:

$$z^1 = \frac{Z^1}{Z^0}, \qquad z^2 = \frac{Z^2}{Z^0}$$

4.2 Fubini-Study Metric

Kähler Potential

$$K = \ln(1 + |z^1|^2 + |z^2|^2) = \ln(1 + z^a \bar{z}^{\bar{a}})$$

where $a, \bar{a} \in \{1, 2\}$ (complex indices) and summation is implied.

Metric from Kähler Potential

$$g_{a\bar{b}} = \partial_a \partial_{\bar{b}} K$$

Step 1: First derivative:

$$\partial_a K = \frac{\bar{z}^{\bar{a}}}{1 + |z|^2}$$ where $|z|^2 \equiv z^c\bar{z}^{\bar{c}} = |z^1|^2 + |z^2|^2$.

Step 2: Second derivative:

$$\partial_{\bar{b}}\partial_a K = \partial_{\bar{b}}\left(\frac{\bar{z}^{\bar{a}}}{1 + |z|^2}\right)$$ $$= \frac{\delta_{a\bar{b}}}{1 + |z|^2} - \frac{\bar{z}^{\bar{a}} z^b}{(1 + |z|^2)^2}$$ $$= \frac{\delta_{a\bar{b}}(1 + |z|^2) - \bar{z}^{\bar{a}} z^b}{(1 + |z|^2)^2}$$

Fubini-Study Metric (Unit Radius)

$$\boxed{\hat{g}_{a\bar{b}} = \frac{(1 + |z|^2)\delta_{a\bar{b}} - \bar{z}_a z_{\bar{b}}}{(1 + |z|^2)^2}}$$

Or equivalently in matrix form (for $\mathbb{CP}^2$):

$$\hat{g}_{a\bar{b}} = \frac{1}{(1+|z|^2)^2}\begin{pmatrix} 1 + |z^2|^2 & -\bar{z}^1 z^2 \\ -z^1\bar{z}^2 & 1 + |z^1|^2 \end{pmatrix}$$

4.3 Real Coordinate Form

Write $z^a = x^a + iy^a$ with real coordinates $(x^1, y^1, x^2, y^2)$.

The real metric $\hat{g}_{mn}$ (where $m, n \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$ labeling $(x^1, y^1, x^2, y^2)$) is:

The factor of 2 comes from $dz\,d\bar{z} = (dx + i\,dy)(dx - i\,dy) = dx^2 + dy^2$.

For $\mathbb{CP}^2$ in $D=4$: Weyl tensor vanishes (conformally flat), so:

$$\hat{R}_{mnpq}\hat{R}^{mnpq} = 2 \times 144 - \frac{576}{3} = 288 - 192 = 96$$

$$\boxed{\begin{aligned} R_{mn}R^{mn} &= \frac{144}{r^4}\\[8pt] R_{mnpq}R^{mnpq} &= \frac{96}{r^4}\\[8pt] R^2 &= \frac{576}{r^4} \end{aligned}}$$

$$R^{a_i}{}_{\mu a_i\nu} = 0$$

$$R_{\mu\nu} = R^{(4)}_{\mu\nu}$$

$$R_{a_i b_i} = R^P{}_{a_i P b_i} = R^\mu{}_{a_i\mu b_i} + R^{c_i}{}_{a_i c_i b_i} + \sum_{j\neq i}R^{a_j}{}_{a_i a_j b_i}$$

(All terms involve mixed Riemann components which vanish for products.)

$$R_{a_i b_j} = 0 \quad \text{for } i \neq j$$

16D Ricci Tensor

$$\boxed{\begin{aligned} R_{\mu\nu} &= R^{(4)}_{\mu\nu}\\[8pt] R_{a_i b_i} &= \frac{6}{r_i^2}g_{a_i b_i}\\[8pt] R_{\mu a_i} &= 0\\[8pt] R_{a_i b_j} &= 0 \quad (i \neq j) \end{aligned}}$$

7. Gauge Field and Flux Calculations

7.1 The Kähler Form on $\mathbb{CP}^2$

The Kähler form $J$ is a closed 2-form defined by:

$$J = ig_{a\bar{b}}dz^a \wedge d\bar{z}^{\bar{b}}$$

In real coordinates:

$$J_{mn} = g_{mp}I^p{}_n$$

where $I^p{}_n$ is the almost complex structure satisfying $I^p{}_m I^m{}_n = -\delta^p_n$.

Properties of the Kähler Form

Closure:

$$dJ = 0$$

Normalization:

$$J \wedge J = 2\,\text{vol}_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$$ $$\int_{\mathbb{CP}^2} J \wedge J = 2\,\text{Vol}(\mathbb{CP}^2) = 8\pi^2 r^4$$

Contraction:

$$J_{mn}J^{mn} = 4$$ $$J_m{}^p J_p{}^n = -\delta_m^n$$

7.2 Gauge Field Ansatz

We introduce three $U(1)$ gauge fields, one for each $\mathbb{CP}^2$:

$$A^{(i)} = A^{(i)}_M dx^M$$

with field strength:

$$F^{(i)} = dA^{(i)} = \frac{1}{2}F^{(i)}_{MN}dx^M \wedge dx^N$$

Monopole/Instanton Ansatz

We take the gauge field proportional to the Kähler potential 1-form:

Step 1: The Kähler potential:

$$K = r_i^2\ln(1 + |z|^2)$$

Step 2: Define the gauge connection:

$$A^{(i)} = \frac{if_i}{2}\left(\partial_a K\, dz^a - \partial_{\bar{a}}K\, d\bar{z}^{\bar{a}}\right)$$ $$= \frac{if_i r_i^2}{2(1+|z|^2)}\left(\bar{z}_a dz^a - z_{\bar{a}}d\bar{z}^{\bar{a}}\right)$$

Step 3: Compute the field strength:

$$F^{(i)} = dA^{(i)} = f_i \cdot ig_{a\bar{b}}dz^a \wedge d\bar{z}^{\bar{b}} = f_i J_i$$

Gauge Field Strength

$$\boxed{F^{(i)}_{mn} = f_i J^{(i)}_{mn}}$$

where $J^{(i)}_{mn}$ is the Kähler form on $\mathbb{CP}^2_i$ and $f_i$ is the flux parameter.

7.3 Field Strength Components

Index Structure

The field strength $F^{(i)}_{MN}$ has non-zero components only on $\mathbb{CP}^2_i$:

$$F^{(i)}_{MN} = \begin{cases} f_i J^{(i)}_{a_i b_i} & M = a_i, N = b_i \in \mathbb{CP}^2_i\\[4pt] 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

Raising Indices

$$F^{(i)MN} = g^{MP}g^{NQ}F^{(i)}_{PQ}$$

For $M, N$ on $\mathbb{CP}^2_i$:

$$F^{(i)a_i b_i} = g^{a_i c_i}g^{b_i d_i}F^{(i)}_{c_i d_i} = \frac{f_i}{r_i^4}\hat{g}^{a_i c_i}\hat{g}^{b_i d_i}J^{(i)}_{c_i d_i} = \frac{f_i}{r_i^4}J^{(i)a_i b_i}$$

7.4 Field Strength Squared

Computing $F^{(i)}_{MN}F^{(i)MN}$

$$F^{(i)}_{MN}F^{(i)MN} = F^{(i)}_{a_i b_i}F^{(i)a_i b_i}$$ $$= f_i J^{(i)}_{a_i b_i} \cdot \frac{f_i}{r_i^4}J^{(i)a_i b_i}$$ $$= \frac{f_i^2}{r_i^4}J^{(i)}_{a_i b_i}J^{(i)a_i b_i}$$

Using $J_{mn}J^{mn} = 4$:

$$F^{(i)}_{MN}F^{(i)MN} = \frac{4f_i^2}{r_i^4}$$
$$\boxed{F^{(i)}_{MN}F^{(i)MN} = \frac{4f_i^2}{r_i^4}}$$

Dirac Quantization Condition

The flux through the $\mathbb{CP}^1 \subset \mathbb{CP}^2$ generator must be quantized:

$$\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb{CP}^1} F^{(i)} = \frac{f_i}{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb{CP}^1} J^{(i)} = \frac{f_i}{2\pi} \cdot 2\pi r_i^2 \cdot \frac{1}{r_i^2} = f_i \in \mathbb{Z}$$

Wait, let me be more careful. For unit-radius $\mathbb{CP}^2$:

$$\int_{\mathbb{CP}^1} \hat{J} = \pi$$

So for radius $r_i$:

$$\int_{\mathbb{CP}^1} J^{(i)} = \pi r_i^2$$

The quantization requires:

$$\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb{CP}^1} F^{(i)} = \frac{f_i \cdot \pi r_i^2}{2\pi} = \frac{f_i r_i^2}{2} = n_i \in \mathbb{Z}$$

Flux Quantization

$$\boxed{f_i = \frac{2n_i}{r_i^2}, \qquad n_i \in \mathbb{Z}}$$

Ansatz Components

8. Stress-Energy Tensor Components

8.1 Gauge Field Stress-Energy

The stress-energy tensor for a $U(1)$ gauge field:

$$T_{MN}^{(F)} = \frac{1}{g^2}\left(F_{MP}F_N{}^P - \frac{1}{4}g_{MN}F_{PQ}F^{PQ}\right)$$

Computing $F^{(i)}_{MP}F^{(i)}_N{}^P$

Case 1: Both indices external ($M = \mu$, $N = \nu$)

$$F^{(i)}_{\mu P}F^{(i)}_\nu{}^P = F^{(i)}_{\mu\rho}F^{(i)}_\nu{}^\rho + F^{(i)}_{\mu a_j}F^{(i)}_\nu{}^{a_j}$$

Since $F^{(i)}$ has support only on $\mathbb{CP}^2_i$:

$$F^{(i)}_{\mu P} = 0 \quad \text{for all } P$$

Therefore:

$$F^{(i)}_{\mu P}F^{(i)}_\nu{}^P = 0$$

Case 2: Both indices on $\mathbb{CP}^2_i$ ($M = a_i$, $N = b_i$)

$$F^{(i)}_{a_i P}F^{(i)}_{b_i}{}^P = F^{(i)}_{a_i c_i}F^{(i)}_{b_i}{}^{c_i}$$ $$= f_i J^{(i)}_{a_i c_i} \cdot \frac{f_i}{r_i^4}J^{(i)}_{b_i}{}^{c_i}$$ $$= \frac{f_i^2}{r_i^4}J^{(i)}_{a_i c_i}J^{(i)c_i}{}_{b_i}$$

Using $J_{ac}J^c{}_b = -\delta_{ab}$ (from $J^2 = -\mathbf{1}$):

$$F^{(i)}_{a_i P}F^{(i)}_{b_i}{}^P = -\frac{f_i^2}{r_i^4}\delta_{a_i b_i} = -\frac{f_i^2}{r_i^4}\cdot\frac{g_{a_i b_i}}{r_i^2} = -\frac{f_i^2}{r_i^6}g_{a_i b_i}$$

Wait, let me recalculate. We have:

$$J^{(i)c_i}{}_{b_i} = g^{c_i d_i}J^{(i)}_{d_i b_i}$$

So:

$$J^{(i)}_{a_i c_i}J^{(i)c_i}{}_{b_i} = J^{(i)}_{a_i c_i}g^{c_i d_i}J^{(i)}_{d_i b_i}$$

In matrix notation: $J \cdot g^{-1} \cdot J$. For Kähler manifolds:

$$J_{ac}g^{cd}J_{db} = -g_{ab}$$

Therefore:

$$F^{(i)}_{a_i c_i}F^{(i)}_{b_i}{}^{c_i} = \frac{f_i^2}{r_i^4}(-g_{a_i b_i}) = -\frac{f_i^2}{r_i^4}g_{a_i b_i}$$

Case 3: Both indices on $\mathbb{CP}^2_j$ with $j \neq i$

$$F^{(i)}_{a_j P}F^{(i)}_{b_j}{}^P = 0$$

(Since $F^{(i)}$ has no components on $\mathbb{CP}^2_j$.)

Assembling the Stress-Energy Tensor

External Components: $T^{(i)}_{\mu\nu}$

$$T^{(i)}_{\mu\nu} = \frac{1}{g_i^2}\left(F^{(i)}_{\mu P}F^{(i)}_\nu{}^P - \frac{1}{4}g_{\mu\nu}F^{(i)}_{PQ}F^{(i)PQ}\right)$$ $$= \frac{1}{g_i^2}\left(0 - \frac{1}{4}g_{\mu\nu}\cdot\frac{4f_i^2}{r_i^4}\right)$$ $$= -\frac{f_i^2}{g_i^2 r_i^4}g_{\mu\nu}$$

Internal Components on $\mathbb{CP}^2_i$: $T^{(i)}_{a_i b_i}$

$$T^{(i)}_{a_i b_i} = \frac{1}{g_i^2}\left(F^{(i)}_{a_i P}F^{(i)}_{b_i}{}^P - \frac{1}{4}g_{a_i b_i}F^{(i)}_{PQ}F^{(i)PQ}\right)$$ $$= \frac{1}{g_i^2}\left(-\frac{f_i^2}{r_i^4}g_{a_i b_i} - \frac{1}{4}g_{a_i b_i}\cdot\frac{4f_i^2}{r_i^4}\right)$$ $$= \frac{1}{g_i^2}\left(-\frac{f_i^2}{r_i^4} - \frac{f_i^2}{r_i^4}\right)g_{a_i b_i}$$ $$= -\frac{2f_i^2}{g_i^2 r_i^4}g_{a_i b_i}$$

Internal Components on $\mathbb{CP}^2_j$ ($j \neq i$): $T^{(i)}_{a_j b_j}$

$$T^{(i)}_{a_j b_j} = \frac{1}{g_i^2}\left(0 - \frac{1}{4}g_{a_j b_j}\cdot\frac{4f_i^2}{r_i^4}\right)$$ $$= -\frac{f_i^2}{g_i^2 r_i^4}g_{a_j b_j}$$

Gauge Field Stress-Energy from $F^{(i)}$

$$\boxed{\begin{aligned} T^{(i)}_{\mu\nu} &= -\frac{f_i^2}{g_i^2 r_i^4}g_{\mu\nu}\\[8pt] T^{(i)}_{a_i b_i} &= -\frac{2f_i^2}{g_i^2 r_i^4}g_{a_i b_i}\\[8pt] T^{(i)}_{a_j b_j} &= -\frac{f_i^2}{g_i^2 r_i^4}g_{a_j b_j} \quad (j \neq i) \end{aligned}}$$

8.2 Total Gauge Stress-Energy

Summing over all three gauge fields:

External:

$$T^{\text{gauge}}_{\mu\nu} = \sum_{i=1}^{3}T^{(i)}_{\mu\nu} = -\sum_{i=1}^{3}\frac{f_i^2}{g_i^2 r_i^4}g_{\mu\nu}$$

On $\mathbb{CP}^2_k$:

$$T^{\text{gauge}}_{a_k b_k} = T^{(k)}_{a_k b_k} + \sum_{j\neq k}T^{(j)}_{a_k b_k}$$ $$= -\frac{2f_k^2}{g_k^2 r_k^4}g_{a_k b_k} - \sum_{j\neq k}\frac{f_j^2}{g_j^2 r_j^4}g_{a_k b_k}$$ $$= -\left(\frac{2f_k^2}{g_k^2 r_k^4} + \sum_{j\neq k}\frac{f_j^2}{g_j^2 r_j^4}\right)g_{a_k b_k}$$

Total Gauge Stress-Energy

$$\boxed{\begin{aligned} T^{\text{gauge}}_{\mu\nu} &= -\left(\sum_{i=1}^{3}\frac{f_i^2}{g_i^2 r_i^4}\right)g_{\mu\nu}\\[10pt] T^{\text{gauge}}_{a_k b_k} &= -\left(\frac{2f_k^2}{g_k^2 r_k^4} + \sum_{j\neq k}\frac{f_j^2}{g_j^2 r_j^4}\right)g_{a_k b_k} \end{aligned}}$$

8.3 Four-Form Flux Stress-Energy

$$T_{MN}^{(G)} = \frac{1}{2 \cdot 3!}\left(G_{MPQR}G_N{}^{PQR} - \frac{1}{8}g_{MN}G_{PQRS}G^{PQRS}\right)$$

Computing $G_{\mu PQR}G_\nu{}^{PQR}$

$$G_{\mu PQR}G_\nu{}^{PQR} = G_{\mu\rho\sigma\tau}G_\nu{}^{\rho\sigma\tau}$$ $$= g_0 \epsilon_{\mu\rho\sigma\tau} \cdot g_0 g^{\rho\alpha}g^{\sigma\beta}g^{\tau\gamma}\epsilon_{\nu\alpha\beta\gamma}$$ $$= g_0^2 \epsilon_{\mu\rho\sigma\tau}\epsilon_\nu{}^{\rho\sigma\tau}$$ $$= g_0^2 \cdot (-6)\delta_{\mu\nu} = -6g_0^2 g_{\mu\nu}$$

Including the trace term with $G^2 = -24g_0^2 + \ldots$:

$$T^{(G)}_{\mu\nu} = \frac{1}{12}\left(-6g_0^2 g_{\mu\nu} - \frac{1}{8}g_{\mu\nu}(-24g_0^2)\right)$$ $$= \frac{1}{12}\left(-6g_0^2 + 3g_0^2\right)g_{\mu\nu}$$ $$= -\frac{g_0^2}{4}g_{\mu\nu}$$

Internal Components

On $\mathbb{CP}^2_k$ (assuming only external $G_4$ flux for simplicity):

$$G_{a_k PQR}G_{b_k}{}^{PQR} = 0$$

So:

$$T^{(G)}_{a_k b_k} = \frac{1}{12}\left(0 - \frac{1}{8}g_{a_k b_k}(-24g_0^2)\right) = \frac{g_0^2}{4}g_{a_k b_k}$$

Freund-Rubin Stress-Energy (External $G_4$ only)

$$\boxed{\begin{aligned} T^{(G)}_{\mu\nu} &= -\frac{g_0^2}{4}g_{\mu\nu}\\[8pt] T^{(G)}_{a_k b_k} &= +\frac{g_0^2}{4}g_{a_k b_k} \end{aligned}}$$

Physical Interpretation: The Freund-Rubin flux acts as a positive cosmological constant in the external space and a negative cosmological constant in the internal space. This is essential for stabilization!

Complete Stress-Energy

$$\boxed{\begin{aligned} T^{\text{total}}_{\mu\nu} &= -\left(\sum_{i=1}^{3}\frac{f_i^2}{g_i^2 r_i^4} + \frac{g_0^2}{4}\right)g_{\mu\nu}\\[12pt] T^{\text{total}}_{a_k b_k} &= -\left(\frac{2f_k^2}{g_k^2 r_k^4} + \sum_{j\neq k}\frac{f_j^2}{g_j^2 r_j^4} - \frac{g_0^2}{4}\right)g_{a_k b_k} \end{aligned}}$$

9. Complete Einstein Equations

9.1 Einstein Tensor Components

Definition

$$G_{MN} = R_{MN} - \frac{1}{2}g_{MN}R$$

External Components: $G_{\mu\nu}$

$$G_{\mu\nu} = R^{(4)}_{\mu\nu} - \frac{1}{2}g_{\mu\nu}R^{(16)}$$ $$= R^{(4)}_{\mu\nu} - \frac{1}{2}g_{\mu\nu}\left(R^{(4)} + \sum_{i=1}^{3}\frac{24}{r_i^2}\right)$$

$$G_{a_k b_k} = R_{a_k b_k} - \frac{1}{2}g_{a_k b_k}R^{(16)}$$ $$= \frac{6}{r_k^2}g_{a_k b_k} - \frac{1}{2}g_{a_k b_k}\left(R^{(4)} + \sum_{i=1}^{3}\frac{24}{r_i^2}\right)$$ $$= \left(\frac{6}{r_k^2} - \frac{1}{2}R^{(4)} - \sum_{i=1}^{3}\frac{12}{r_i^2}\right)g_{a_k b_k}$$

9.2 Full Field Equations

LHS:

$$G_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu} + \alpha_2 \mathcal{E}^{(2)}_{\mu\nu} + \ldots$$

RHS:

10. Stabilization Conditions

10.1 Symmetric Solution: $r_1 = r_2 = r_3 = r$

Assume all three $\mathbb{CP}^2$ factors have the same radius $r$ and flux $f$ (with coupling $g$):

External Equation:

$$\Lambda_4 = \Lambda - \frac{36}{r^2} + \kappa_{16}^2\left(\frac{3f^2}{g^2 r^4} + \frac{g_0^2}{4}\right)$$

Internal Equation (any $k$):

$$\frac{6}{r^2} - 2\Lambda_4 - \frac{36}{r^2} + \Lambda = -\kappa_{16}^2\left(\frac{2f^2}{g^2 r^4} + \frac{2f^2}{g^2 r^4} - \frac{g_0^2}{4}\right)$$ $$-\frac{30}{r^2} - 2\Lambda_4 + \Lambda = -\kappa_{16}^2\left(\frac{4f^2}{g^2 r^4} - \frac{g_0^2}{4}\right)$$

Substitute $\Lambda_4$:

$$-\frac{30}{r^2} - 2\left(\Lambda - \frac{36}{r^2} + \kappa_{16}^2\left(\frac{3f^2}{g^2 r^4} + \frac{g_0^2}{4}\right)\right) + \Lambda = -\kappa_{16}^2\left(\frac{4f^2}{g^2 r^4} - \frac{g_0^2}{4}\right)$$ $$-\frac{30}{r^2} - 2\Lambda + \frac{72}{r^2} - \frac{6\kappa_{16}^2 f^2}{g^2 r^4} - \frac{\kappa_{16}^2 g_0^2}{2} + \Lambda = -\frac{4\kappa_{16}^2 f^2}{g^2 r^4} + \frac{\kappa_{16}^2 g_0^2}{4}$$ $$\frac{42}{r^2} - \Lambda = -\frac{4\kappa_{16}^2 f^2}{g^2 r^4} + \frac{6\kappa_{16}^2 f^2}{g^2 r^4} + \frac{\kappa_{16}^2 g_0^2}{4} + \frac{\kappa_{16}^2 g_0^2}{2}$$ $$\frac{42}{r^2} - \Lambda = \frac{2\kappa_{16}^2 f^2}{g^2 r^4} + \frac{3\kappa_{16}^2 g_0^2}{4}$$

Stabilization Equation (Symmetric Case)

$$\boxed{\frac{42}{r^2} - \Lambda = \frac{2\kappa_{16}^2 f^2}{g^2 r^4} + \frac{3\kappa_{16}^2 g_0^2}{4}}$$

This is a quadratic equation in $1/r^2$. Setting $x = 1/r^2$:

$$42x - \Lambda = \frac{2\kappa_{16}^2 f^2}{g^2}x^2 + \frac{3\kappa_{16}^2 g_0^2}{4}$$ $$\frac{2\kappa_{16}^2 f^2}{g^2}x^2 - 42x + \Lambda + \frac{3\kappa_{16}^2 g_0^2}{4} = 0$$

10.2 Solution for Radius

Using the quadratic formula with $A = \frac{2\kappa_{16}^2 f^2}{g^2}$, $B = -42$, $C = \Lambda + \frac{3\kappa_{16}^2 g_0^2}{4}$:

$$x = \frac{42 \pm \sqrt{42^2 - 4 \cdot \frac{2\kappa_{16}^2 f^2}{g^2}\left(\Lambda + \frac{3\kappa_{16}^2 g_0^2}{4}\right)}}{2 \cdot \frac{2\kappa_{16}^2 f^2}{g^2}}$$ $$\frac{1}{r^2} = \frac{g^2}{4\kappa_{16}^2 f^2}\left[42 \pm \sqrt{1764 - \frac{8\kappa_{16}^2 f^2}{g^2}\left(\Lambda + \frac{3\kappa_{16}^2 g_0^2}{4}\right)}\right]$$

Stabilized Radius

$$\boxed{r^2 = \frac{4\kappa_{16}^2 f^2}{g^2\left[42 \pm \sqrt{1764 - \frac{8\kappa_{16}^2 f^2}{g^2}\left(\Lambda + \frac{3\kappa_{16}^2 g_0^2}{4}\right)}\right]}}$$

10.3 Effective 4D Cosmological Constant

Substituting the stabilized $r$ back into the external equation:

$$\Lambda_4 = \Lambda - \frac{36}{r^2} + \kappa_{16}^2\left(\frac{3f^2}{g^2 r^4} + \frac{g_0^2}{4}\right)$$

Conditions for de Sitter ($\Lambda_4 > 0$), Minkowski ($\Lambda_4 = 0$), or Anti-de Sitter ($\Lambda_4 < 0$)

The sign of $\Lambda_4$ depends on the balance between:

  • $\Lambda$ (bulk cosmological constant)
  • $-36/r^2$ (negative contribution from internal curvature)
  • $\kappa_{16}^2 f^2/(g^2 r^4)$ (positive contribution from gauge flux)
  • $\kappa_{16}^2 g_0^2/4$ (positive contribution from Freund-Rubin flux)
